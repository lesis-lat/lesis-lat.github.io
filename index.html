LESIS is an applied security research entity focused on developing effective offensive technologies in support of critical operations. Its approach is grounded in a thorough attacker's perspective and rigorous, research-driven thinking.

VULNERABILITY RESEARCH

A detailed analysis of the attack surface of the product under test, taking into consideration the security expectations, objectives and threat model. Our approach aims to verify the resilience of all components of the product under test - typically composed of architecture review, infrastructure testing, application security, code review and reverse engineering, as well as the creation of bespoke tools such as fuzzers and custom code static analyzers.

RESEARCH & DEVELOPMENT

Hard problems require unique solutions. We design and build tailor-made tools for optimizing operations, providing exclusive capabilities and on-demand research to improve exploitation and post-exploitation tasks. From high-level design to low-level implementation, our process is driven by the specific demands of each engagement. Over the past years, our team has worked on projects involving static analysis, fuzzing, data mining, and custom instrumentation.

OUR APPROACH

At LESIS (Laboratory of Engineering Studies in Information Security), our focus is on applied research in offensive security technologies that underpin critical operations. Our team of engineers and researchers is dedicated to uncovering vulnerabilities, developing innovative solutions, and strengthening the security of organizations facing complex cybersecurity challenges.

With a deep understanding of the tactics, techniques, and procedures (TTPs) employed by attackers, we offer a unique perspective on protecting systems and infrastructures. We work closely with our collaborators, tailoring strategies to meet specific needs and objectives. Whether through vulnerability research, advanced exploitation techniques, or the development of custom tools for security assessments, our aim is to reinforce digital resilience and empower those involved to stay ahead of emerging threats.

Our commitment is to deliver world-class research, cutting-edge technology, and actionable insights that contribute to a safer digital landscape. At LESIS, we do more than assess security—we propel it forward.
