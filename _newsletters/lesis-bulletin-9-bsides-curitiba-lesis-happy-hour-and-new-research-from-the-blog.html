Research & Articles

Working the cloud: IP rotation with serverless computing

In a new post on the LESIS Blog, Lucas Vieira Araujo introduces cf-proxy, a tool built from a practical need encountered during penetration testing.

The article explores how Cloudflare Workers can be used as a lightweight rotating proxy, changing the source IP between requests and helping in scenarios involving IP-based rate limits, blocked addresses, and network filtering. It also goes through how the tool works, the problem that led to its creation, and some of the limitations of using Cloudflare’s infrastructure this way.
