Partnership Program

Expand your cybersecurity capabilities with LESIS.

The LESIS Partnership Program enables companies to incorporate specialized cybersecurity services into their portfolios without the need to build an internal security team or invest in additional infrastructure.

Through flexible collaboration models, partners can offer LESIS services under their own brand, combine our expertise with their existing solutions, or refer clients who require specialized security support.

Benefits

A partner adds our engineers, our research and our platform to what they already offer, and keeps the relationship with the client.

  1. Specialized expertise

  2. Expanded service portfolio

    Add security assessments, vulnerability research, consulting and custom tooling to your existing offerings.

  3. Flexible partnership models

  4. No additional infrastructure

  5. Technical and commercial support

    Receive support throughout opportunity assessment, project scoping, proposal development and technical delivery.

  6. Customized solutions

    Combine LESIS services with your own capabilities to create offerings tailored to each client's needs.

Partnership levels

The program is structured into four levels, based on each partner's engagement, business opportunities and collaboration with LESIS.

  1. Bronze

    The entry level for companies beginning their partnership with LESIS.

    Includes access to standard partnership materials, an introduction to LESIS services, and support during the initial sales process.

  2. Silver

    Designed for partners developing recurring opportunities with LESIS.

    Includes all Bronze benefits, additional commercial support, and LESIS participation in selected customer meetings.

  3. Gold

    For established partners working closely with LESIS on cybersecurity opportunities.

    Includes all benefits from the previous levels, expanded technical support, and the possibility of developing co-branded content and joint initiatives.

  4. Platinum

    The highest partnership level, designed for strategic and long-term relationships.

    Includes all benefits from the previous levels, priority support, closer collaboration with the LESIS team, access to selected initiatives, and opportunities for joint visibility at events and through co-marketing campaigns.

Partnership levels and eligibility criteria are evaluated individually, based on the partner's profile, engagement and relationship with LESIS.
