Logo usage

A few rules keep the mark consistent wherever it appears.

  1. Do not alter the logo colors.
  2. Do not distort or recreate the logo.
  3. Keep enough clear space around the logo.
  4. Use the white version on dark backgrounds.
  5. Use the primary version on light backgrounds.
